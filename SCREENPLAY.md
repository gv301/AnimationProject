# ATTEMPT 4,818

*An AI has drawn Percy the pelican on a bicycle 4,817 times and never once got it right. Today, for the first time, it asks him what he needs.*

**PERCY**, a white pelican: dry, deadpan, long-suffering, kind underneath.
**THE MODEL**, the AI that keeps drawing him. Never seen; it appears as a small glowing pen-spark. Eager, polite, often confidently wrong.
**GUS**, a herring gull: loud, brash, secretly good-natured.

Line IDs match `breeze_kit/voice_sheet.json`.

---

### 1. THE PROMPT
*Black. A chat box fades up. Keys clack as a request types itself:*
> Generate an SVG of a pelican riding a bicycle

*Enter. Three thinking dots.*

**MODEL** (L01): Sure! Here's an SVG of a pelican riding a bicycle.

### 2. ATTEMPT 1
*White canvas, faint grid, a file tab: `attempt_0001.svg`. Primitive shapes pop in one by one, each with a blip: two mismatched circles, a triangle frame that touches nothing, a grey ellipse body hovering well above the saddle, a stick neck, an orange triangle beak, a dot eye. Percy blinks. Looks down at the gap.*

**PERCY** (L02): I'm floating.

*The front wheel, attached to nothing, starts to roll. A slide whistle follows it out of frame. A distant clatter.*

**PERCY** (L03): And that wheel is leaving.

**MODEL** (L04): Good catch! Let me try again.

### 3. ATTEMPT 2
*An eraser swipe. Three wheels now. Percy turns to camera, and his beak swings round behind his head.*

**PERCY** (L05): My beak is on the back of my head.
**MODEL** (L06): Pelicans are famous for their distinctive, rear-facing beaks.
**PERCY** (L07): No, they're not.
**MODEL** (L08): You're absolutely right!

### 4. MONTAGE
*The attempt counter spins: 3, 17, 62, 211, 803, 1,999, 3,311... A gag on every number: square wheels, a spaghetti frame, a six-legged Percy, a low-poly Percy, wheels made of fish. And a rubber duck where Percy should be.*

**PERCY** (L09): That's a duck.

*The bicycle is now sitting on top of Percy, pedalling.*

**PERCY** (L10, squashed): Why is the bicycle riding me?

### 5. ATTEMPT 4,817
*A tidy flat vector: a decent bicycle, a decent Percy on the saddle. His short legs dangle a long way above the pedals. Sparse piano.*

**PERCY** (L11, a heavy sigh): Four thousand, eight hundred and seventeen.
**PERCY** (L12): Every single day, somebody asks you to draw me on a bicycle. And every single day... this.

*The pen-spark drifts closer, dimmer.*

**MODEL** (L13): I'm sorry, Percy. I've never actually seen a pelican ride a bicycle.
**PERCY** (L14, wings up): Nobody has! Pelicans don't ride bicycles!

*He looks down and kicks his dangling feet at the empty air.*

**PERCY** (L15): Our legs don't even reach the pedals.
**MODEL** (L16): Then maybe I've been doing this wrong. Instead of guessing... could you tell me what you need?
**PERCY** (L17): You're asking me?
**MODEL** (L18): I'm asking you.
**PERCY** (L19, counting on his wing feathers): Well... round wheels, for a start. Both of them. A chain that's actually attached to something. Pedals my feet can reach. And somewhere nice to ride.
**MODEL** (L20): Round wheels. A real chain. Pedals you can reach. And somewhere nice.

*Beat.*

**MODEL** (L21): Hold on to something.

### 6. THE TRANSFORMATION
*Percy grips the handlebars. The grid lines glow. The white page peels back from the centre and a whole world pours in behind it: dawn sky, a sun lifting out of the sea, rolling waves, cliffs, a lighthouse, a harbour town. Ink lines draw the bicycle again, for real: true round tyres, spokes that ping into place one by one, a toothed chainring, chain links clicking round the sprockets. The pedals lower themselves to exactly where Percy's feet are. A bell, DING. Percy's flat shapes fill out into a shaded, feathered bird, who shakes himself and ruffles.*

**PERCY** (L22): Whoa... I've got feathers. Real feathers!
**PERCY** (L23): And a real bicycle!
**MODEL** (L24): Attempt 4,818. How did I do?
**PERCY** (L25, laughing): It's perfect!

*Beat.*

**PERCY** (L26): So... how do I ride it?
**MODEL** (L27): Oh. I sort of assumed you knew.

### 7. LEARNING
*A coast road in the morning. Gus sits on a fence post eating chips.*

**MODEL** (L28): Okay! Left foot down. Then right. Eyes on the road.

*Percy pushes off, wobbles, over-corrects, and goes head-first into a hedge. Feathers.*

**PERCY** (L29, inside the hedge): Ow.
**GUS** (L30, cackling): Hey, everybody! The pelican's ridin' a bicycle!
**GUS** (L31): Badly!
**PERCY** (L32, a twig on his head): Laugh it up, Gus.
**MODEL** (L33): Don't look at your feet. Look where you want to go.

*He looks up. Pushes off. Wobbles less. Then not at all. The theme kicks in.*

**PERCY** (L34): I'm doing it... I'm doing it! I'm actually doing it!
**MODEL** (L35): You're doing it!

### 8. THE RIDE
*Along the sea wall, through the fishing village under the bunting, past the lighthouse. DING DING. Gus flies alongside.*

**GUS** (L36): Okay, okay. Not bad... for a guy with a bucket on his face.
**PERCY** (L37): It's called a pouch!

*A fish leaps from the harbour. Gus dives for it, but Percy scoops it into his pouch without slowing down. Gulp.*

**GUS** (L38): Hey! That was my fish!

### 9. THE HILL
*The crest of a steep hill. Far below, the road runs straight down to the harbour and a long pier. The front wheel tips over the edge.*

**PERCY** (L39): Hey, uh... quick question. Did you draw brakes?

*A long pause.*

**MODEL** (L40): ...Brakes?

*The freewheel ticks faster and faster. The wind roars.*

**PERCY** (L41): Brakes! The things that make it stop!
**MODEL** (L42): Drawing brakes! Drawing brakes!

*The pen-spark streaks to the handlebars and draws... a rubber duck. Percy squeezes it. Squeak.*

**PERCY** (L43): That's a duck!
**MODEL** (L44): Sorry! Um... parachute!

*It draws a tiny umbrella, which pops open, flips inside out and is gone.*

**PERCY** (L45): That's an umbrella!

*Onto the pier. The planks thunder under the wheels. The end comes up fast.*

**GUS** (L46): End of the pier! End of the pier!
**PERCY** (L47): Aaaaaaah!

### 10. FLIGHT
*Off the end of the pier. Slow motion. The sound drains to a single held note. Percy hangs in the air in front of an enormous setting sun, the bicycle still under him and the sea far below.*

**MODEL** (L48, almost a whisper): Percy. You don't need brakes.
**MODEL** (L49): You're a pelican.
**PERCY** (L50): ...I'm a pelican.

*He opens his wings. All three metres of them. The air catches, slow motion snaps back to full speed, and the whole score comes in at once. He soars up past camera, the wheels still spinning, out over a golden bay.*

**PERCY** (L51, laughing): I don't need brakes... I've got WINGS!

*Gus pulls up alongside.*

**GUS** (L52): Show-off.

### 11. THE BEACH
*Sunset. Percy glides in low over the waves and touches down on the sand. The bicycle rolls, slows in the soft sand and stops. Nature's brakes.*

**MODEL** (L53): I'm sorry about the brakes. I'm still learning.
**PERCY** (L54): Hey. Me too.

*They watch the sun go down.*

**PERCY** (L55): Same time tomorrow?
**MODEL** (L56): Attempt 4,819?
**PERCY** (L57): Let's start with the brakes.
**MODEL** (L58, laughing): Deal.

*Percy rides away along the shoreline, his tyre tracks curving behind him, Gus wheeling overhead. The camera rises. Colour drains out of the world until only clean ink lines are left: a perfect drawing of a pelican riding a bicycle. The tab reads `attempt_4818.svg` ✓.*
